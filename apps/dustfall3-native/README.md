# Dustfall III — native iOS

A Swift rewrite of `dustfall-3/index.html`, built alongside the web-wrapped app in `apps/dustfall3-ios`. No web view: the rules are in `Sources/Game.swift` and every shape is drawn with CoreGraphics in `Sources/Draw.swift`.

**Stage 1: the first town.** Ride, toss coins, hire drifters, rebuild the store, saloon and sheriff's office, raise the two gates, make deputies and prospectors, collect the morning payout, and hold off the night raids with the six-shooter. Strongboxes, coin refunds, one building under construction at a time, and the web game's costs, payouts, day length and raid sizes are carried over.

**Stage 2 (this build): the railroad.** Rebuild the station and the rails are laid from the east; the train rolls in and waits. A ticket paid at the passenger car takes you to the next town down the line (Dustfall, Redwater, Last Hope, Cold Creek, Bitter End) with half of purse and bank, never under 8 nor over 25. Prices rise a quarter per town and raids start a night harder. The stable (a faster horse) and the bank (deposits raiders cannot touch, a little interest each dawn) open from the second town on, as in the web game. The game saves itself at every dawn, when something is built, and whenever the app is left; the title screen offers Continue. Controls: a coin button and a gun button, both repeating while held.

**Stage 3 (this build): the whole town.** The town centre (square, then clock tower) caps how far buildings and gates can be improved, and the station cannot be rebuilt until every building is open and the clock tower is up, as in the web game. Barbed wire outside each gate (slows, cuts, then a sharpshooter), the Gatling gun on a third-level gate, and the buildings that arrive down the line: bordello and doctor at the third town, church and blacksmith at the fourth, telegraph at the last. From the second town the east side sits 340 further out, as in the web game. Sheriff levels and the blacksmith improve deputies; the stable and blacksmith speed the horse.

**Stage 4 (this build): ranch, claim and outlaws.** From the second town: a rope rack beside the stable turns a townsman into a rancher, ranchers raise calves in the pasture east of town (two head each, three with the bigger stable, twelve at most) and each grown cow pays 2 coins at dawn; the old claim out west can be staked (4 coins a dawn unless raiders came from the west) and guarded (7 a dawn, always). Every third night from the fourth an outlaw boss rides in with a bounty of 8, and he shoots deputies out of their badges unless there is a doctor; every fifth night from the eighth a gang of horsemen charges one end of town. Racks now cost 1 coin, as in the web game (earlier native builds charged 2).

**Stage 5 (this build): country and weather.** Each town has its own country with the web game's colours: Dustfall and Last Hope are desert, Redwater is a river valley with trees and a ford east of town that slows everyone who crosses, Cold Creek and Bitter End are snow country with pines and snow on the roofs, where raiders on foot are slowed and cattle will not calve. The weather is rolled each dawn with the web game's odds: dust storms (blizzards in the snow) cut how far deputies and guns can see and hide raiders until they are close; rain and snowfall; thunderstorms that build for the afternoon or the night, with lightning that now and then finds a raider outside the gates. Rain grows the herd.

**Stage 6: the rider, the map and the forks.** The horse and rider are redrawn in detail (jointed legs with walk and gallop gaits, mane, tail, bridle, saddle, stirrups, scarf, hat, gunbelt). The line forks as in the web game: Redwater or Iron Gulch (a canyon with one way in, from the east), Last Hope or Timberline (pine country), Cold Creek or Silver Peak, then Bitter End. A territory map shows the way you came and asks at each fork; it is also on the pause menu.

**Stage 7 (this build): the stage line, wanted men, duels, floods and strays.** The stagecoach comes through every third day from the second town on: pay for a shotgun guard while it waters by the saloon and the line pays you at dawn; unguarded, road agents hold it up at the rock arch and run with the strongbox. Wanted posters put a man at the old mission or the rock arch for three days. Every fifth day a gunman calls you out for a duel at high noon, with the web game's swinging marker. A thunderstorm fills the wash east of town until the next night and leaves gold in the gravel. Stray longhorns graze past the rock arch and follow you to the pasture or into town to sell.

**Stage 8 (this build): the open west, and the story.** The world now runs 700 further west, as in the web game. Out there: the trading post (dynamite, a speed loader, a signal lantern, one item a day), the scout camp (three days of scouts who name the raid's side and ambush two raiders a night), the ghost town (pay for a day's salvage every couple of days), the oil seep (raise a derrick that pays at the town centre each dawn, until lightning sets it alight) and wild mustangs to rope at a gallop and sell in town. East of town, the cavalry outpost sends a patrol against the night's raiders; west of town, a medicine show pulls up every fourth day. The story scenes play as pictures with a heading and a line, tapped through: the badge, the brand, Crane's telegram, his hired gun, the rail line, his private train, and the end. A town earns its star when nothing in it can be improved further.

**Stage 9 (this build): fire, the yard, and the strange things.** Raiders carry torches and lightning strikes roofs: a burning building is fought by hand, quickly by the water tower's fire brigade, and prevented by the windmill's lightning rods; left 22 seconds it burns out, costs up to 4 coins and pays nothing the next day. Thunder can stampede the herd east until you ride past and turn it. After dark a coin patches a damaged gate, and four hire a rifleman onto a gatehouse roof (up to three). A coin at the church rings the bell and puts the deputies on the gates until dawn. Past the far east camp lies the old rail yard: dead engines, a hobo jungle that sells rumours, and a sealed boxcar worth opening every few days. On a clear night something may come down for a cow, a stray or a mustang, and a shot while the beam is on drives it off; some nights a train that is not there runs through the yard.

**Stage 10 (this build): the last rules.** The horse has wind: fresh, it gallops 28% faster for about five seconds (nine with new shoes), then drops to its usual pace until it has stood, walked or watered at a trough; a sack of oats or a lucky tonic stops it tiring for the day, and up to three mustangs broken at the stable each make it tire a quarter slower. Being caught with an empty purse costs 5 from the bank, and with nothing in either you are run out of town: game over. Raiders run townsfolk off (or into the doctor's care until dawn, or not at all once the bell has them indoors), rustle cattle, and an outlaw boss robs a first-level bank; the bank's second level cannot be robbed and pays up to 6 a day. The hobos can be hired like any drifter, and the saucer will take a raider's horse from under him.

Still different from the web game: the land is flat everywhere, the map is a plain list of stops rather than a drawn territory, raiders only rob you when you ride into one (they do not chase you), and the reach and aim of deputies, guns, cavalry and lightning are approximations.

Not ported: the handcar spur (removed from the web game too) and the cave (switched off in the web game).

- Same bundle ID as the web-wrapped app (`com.brianward.dustfall3`, team `55NAZA6UKE`), so a finished native build can replace it in the same App Store record. Until then it uploads as **version 1.1** with build numbers from 101, so it sits in its own TestFlight group and cannot be confused with the 1.0 builds in review.
- The Xcode project is generated from `project.yml` by XcodeGen, so there is no `.xcodeproj` in the repo.
- Build: the "Dustfall III Native iOS" workflow runs on every push to this branch that touches this folder. It compiles on a GitHub Mac, takes simulator screenshots, uploads to TestFlight, and writes the result to the `dustfall3-native-buildlog` branch.
