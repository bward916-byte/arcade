Common questions

  • How do I play? Drag anywhere on the screen to steer the marble. Reach the goal before the clock runs out. The clock starts when you first move.
  • How do I pause or turn the sound off? Tap the pause button at the top left. Music, sound and vibration each have their own switch there.
  • Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • How do I get the other marbles? They unlock as you clear levels. Tap the marble row on the title screen to switch.
  • Does it need the internet? No. It plays fully offline.

Privacy policy

Marble Descent does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • The game makes no network connections while you play.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

If you email for support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you.
