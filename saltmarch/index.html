Push the stick to ride; push it all the way and the horse breaks into a gallop (keys: A/D, hold to gallop). Your horse waits at the shore; the ship takes you across. Ride into a town to manage it, over crates and chests to open them.
Towns bank gold while you're away. Raids come every half minute or so: a town with less defense than the raid is overrun and stops earning until you ride back and retake it by the blade — sometimes past a champion.
