Who does what

    Releasing someone makes them a plain villager again, free to build.

    Paused

    Ride left and right. Toss coins to nomads at their tents and they join you. Toss coins at ruins and your villagers rebuild them. Coins on a tool rack turn villagers into diggers, guards and herders. Raiders come after dark: loose arrows, and never be caught with an empty purse. Raise the obelisk, build the dock, and sail on when you choose.

    Your town is saved on this device as you play. If the game runs slowly, set Graphics to reduced or minimal.
