Common questions

  • How do I play? Push the stick to ride left or right. Tap the coin button to toss a coin at drifters, lots and buildings. After dark, tap the gun button to fire at raiders.
  • How do I pause or turn the sound off? Tap Menu at the top right.
  • Where is my game saved? On your iPhone only. It is saved every dawn and whenever you leave the game. Deleting the app deletes the save.
  • The screen says to turn my phone. The game plays sideways. Turn your phone to landscape.
  • Does it need the internet? No. It plays fully offline.

Privacy policy

Dustfall III does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • The game makes no network connections while you play.
  • Your saved game and settings stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

If you email for support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you.
